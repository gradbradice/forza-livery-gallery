## English

This is a beta: there's a lot that's new in it, and some things may not work as they should. If you spot a bug, please let us know.

**Forza Horizon 5, 4 and 3**

* FH5, FH4 and FH3 are now in the gallery, in the game switcher next to FH6. Liveries are read from Steam saves (FH5 and FH4) and Microsoft Store saves. In this version the liveries of these games are only shown: the Trash, the Storage, returning to the game and restoring from a backup are not available yet, and the status bar shows "View only".
* Each game has its own livery limit: 1000 in FH5, 500 in FH4 and FH3.
* 3D and drawn previews work for these games too. If the game's files can't be read (the encrypted Microsoft Store version, or the game isn't installed), the livery is drawn with the files of another installed game. The gallery says which one: car models, logos and paints may then look a little different.
* "Find in game" and the "As in the game" sort work in FH5, FH4 and FH3.
* Author cards are now shared by all games.
* The livery window has a new "Move to the storage" button.
* "Settings → Find in game → Wait after opening “My Designs”": how long to wait for the game to open the menu again before moving the cursor, from 0 to 10 seconds, separately for each game.
* The livery server works with FH5, FH4 and FH3 too. If the server can't decrypt the saves of a game or of its version, the server indicator shows "Server: this game is not supported yet", and the gallery checks again by itself every half hour.
* The livery server connection is now one for all games: you agree once in "Settings → Livery server". If you had already turned the server on for FH6, it's turned on for FH5, FH4 and FH3 too.

**Cards and the window**

* Cards come in three sizes: small, normal and large. Switch between them with the squares on the right of the status bar, Ctrl with the mouse wheel, or Ctrl+"+", Ctrl+"−" and Ctrl+0. Cards fill the whole row. A small card shows only the essentials, and its buttons appear when you hover over it.
* A group's header stays at the top of the list while the group scrolls.
* Interface scale: "Settings → Appearance → Interface scale", from 100 to 200%.
* While a preview loads, the card is filled with the preview's average color instead of gray.

**Properties panel and selection**

* There's a properties panel on the right now: the "Properties" button in the window's title bar, or Ctrl+I. It shows the selected livery's picture, name, car and author, its tags, its note and action buttons. With several liveries selected, the panel shows what they have in common, and the note can be added to or replaced for all of them.
* The Archive is now called the Trash: in menus, windows, tooltips and settings. Liveries that were already in the Archive are still there: only the name has changed.
* With several liveries selected and the panel closed, a bar appears at the bottom: favorite, lock, tags, storage, "More actions" and "Clear selection".
* Tags are now edited in one place. The field suggests the tags already in the gallery, each with its number of liveries. A tag that only some of the selected liveries have is shown dashed with "3 of 5": click it to add it to all of them. The T key opens the tag field.

**Messages**

* After a livery is moved, or several liveries are changed at once, a message with an "Undo" button appears and stays for 8 seconds. Ctrl+Z still undoes the same action.
* An empty gallery explains why it's empty and offers a way out: "Clear the search", "Reset the filters", "Choose a folder", "Check again" or "Show the game's liveries".

**Screenshots**

* Screenshots can be shown "With captions" or as "Tiles". They have their own three sizes, separate from the cards.

**Keyboard and accessibility**

* Tab moves through the toolbar into the list. Shift+F10 or the context menu key opens the menu of the card under the cursor.
* Text colors in the light and dark themes have slightly more contrast.

**Reliability**

* If the file of tags, notes, favorites, locks or livery numbers is in use by another program at startup (such as OneDrive sync or an antivirus), the gallery tries to open it a few more times.
* After a filter is removed, the list goes back exactly where it was.
