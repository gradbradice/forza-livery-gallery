## English

The program is now called Forza Livery Gallery (formerly FH6 Livery Gallery). On first launch it moves your settings, archive, backups and the rest of your data over by itself, and 1.6.0 keeps working as before.

* The gallery can now be installed, and from then on it updates itself: it finds a new version, downloads it in the background and installs it when you close the gallery, or right away with "Restart and update". Only versions signed by the author are installed. Beta versions can be turned on in "Settings → Gallery → Updates". Coming from 1.6.0, you need to download the installer by hand once.
* New livery storage: keep liveries outside the game's save. Choose its folder in "Settings → Folders", on another drive if you like, and move it later. Liveries you put into that folder by hand are picked up by the gallery automatically.
* Each livery can now have its own screenshots, easy to keep and browse right in the gallery. Any screenshot can be used as the cover instead of the game's preview.
* Updated 3D view: put tuning parts on the car, wheels included, and hide the license plate.
* Each account now has its own archive and its own backups. If 1.6.0 didn't record which account an entry belongs to, the gallery offers to move it to the right one.
* The "On cars" filter can now show the liveries not used on any car first, or only those. The filter works once the list from the livery server is available.
* Some FH6 players have a livery from the developers that is kept on the game's servers rather than in the save and always comes first in "My Designs". Set how many such liveries you have in "Settings → Find in game", and finding a livery in the game will point to the right slot again.

* リバリーごとにスクリーンショットを持てるようになり、ギャラリー内で手軽に保存・閲覧できます。どのスクリーンショットも、ゲームのプレビューの代わりにカバーにできます。

* FH6 では、開発元から配布されたリバリーがセーブデータではなくゲームのサーバーに保存され、「マイデザイン」で常に先頭に並ぶことがあります。その数を「設定 → ゲーム内で探す」で指定すると、ゲーム内でリバリーを探したときに再び正しい位置を示します。
