## English

* The livery server has moved to a new address and has a new certificate. Gallery 1.7.2 connects to it by itself. Versions up to and including 1.7.1 cannot connect to the new server.
* The gallery starts faster, and right after it starts a small "Starting…" window appears until the main window opens.
* Only one gallery runs at a time now. Starting it again brings the window of the one already open to the front.
* The tag strip above the gallery can now be made lower: drag the line under it. Its height changes by whole rows. The tags the gallery is currently filtered by come first, so they are always visible even in a low strip. With 30 tags or more, a "Find a tag" box appears at the start of the strip.

* Галерея запускается быстрее, а сразу после запуска появляется окошко «Запуск…», пока не откроется главное окно.

## 简体中文
